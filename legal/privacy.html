  • بيانات قياس الأداء عبر Firebase Performance Monitoring في نسخة الإنتاج، مثل زمن بدء التطبيق ومدد العمليات وأنماط طلبات الشبكة بصورة مجمّعة؛ لا نرفق بهذه القياسات معرّفات العملاء أو الطلبات أو العناوين، ولا نجمع محتوى الطلبات أو معاملات عناوين URL.
  • الموقع الجغرافي: لو ضغطت «تحديد موقعي» نستخدم موقع جهازك مرة واحدة لتحديد عنوان التوصيل، ونحفظ العنوان وإحداثياته في حسابك لتسهيل طلباتك. لا نتتبّع موقعك في الخلفية.

  • رمز التسليم: رمز من 4 أرقام يظهر لك وحدك داخل التطبيق أثناء توصيل طلبك، وتعطيه للمندوب بعد الاستلام لتأكيد التسليم. الرمز لا يُخزَّن في قاعدة البيانات ولا يراه المندوب أو المحل، ونسجّل فقط عدد المحاولات الخاطئة لمنع التخمين ووقت تأكيد التسليم.
  • بلاغات تعذر التسليم: إذا تعذر التسليم يسجّل المندوب السبب (مثل عدم الرد أو عنوان غير صحيح)، ويظهر السبب لك وللمحل وللإدارة.

  • «عندي مشكلة في الطلب» (اختياري): لو بلّغت عن مشكلة في طلب نسجّل نوع المشكلة وتفاصيلها اللي كتبتها (حتى 300 حرف) مع رقم الطلب والمحل، ويراها فريق الإدارة بس لمتابعتها، وتُحذف عند حذف الحساب.

بيانات أصحاب المحلات والمناديب والمشرفين والإدارة

إذا أنشأت حسابًا لصاحب محل أو مندوب أو مشرف، أو مُنحت صلاحية إدارية، فقد نعالج بالإضافة إلى بيانات الحساب بيانات الدور اللازمة لتشغيل الخدمة: اسم النشاط وبيانات المحل وعنوانه وصوره ومنتجاته وأسعاره لصاحب المحل؛ الاسم ورقم الهاتف وبيانات المركبة وطلبات التوصيل والتسليم والتسويات والتقييمات للمندوب؛ بيانات الإشراف على المناديب للمشرف؛ وسجلات الإجراءات الإدارية والوصول إلى الطلبات والشكاوى للدور الإداري. تُستخدم هذه البيانات لإدارة الطلبات والتوصيل والتسويات والدعم ومنع إساءة الاستخدام.

صاحب المحل لا يطّلع على عنوان العميل أو رقم هاتفه أو بريده من مستند الطلب العام. المندوب لا يحصل على بيانات الاتصال إلا للطلب المكلّف به وخلال مرحلة التوصيل المسموح بها. المشرف يرى بيانات المناديب المرتبطين به بالقدر اللازم للإشراف. الإدارة المخوّلة يمكنها الوصول إلى بيانات التشغيل اللازمة لحل المشكلات ومتابعة الخدمة، وتُقيّد صلاحياتها بحسب الدور.

عند حذف حساب صاحب محل أو مندوب أو مشرف أو مسؤول، تُزال صلاحياته وملفات دوره ورموز أجهزته، وتُعالج الطلبات والسجلات المرتبطة به بالحذف أو إخفاء الهوية بحسب الحاجة المحاسبية والتشغيلية. إذا كان صاحب المحل هو المالك النشط الأخير، نوقف ظهور المحل ونحتفظ مؤقتًا ببيانات المحل والكتالوج لإعادة تعيينها أو حذفها إداريًا. وقد نحتفظ بسجلات مالية أو تشغيلية مجهّلة أو لازمة قانونًا للمدة التي يفرضها القانون.

مدة الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

نحتفظ ببيانات حسابك وطلباتك طالما حسابك قائم. وعند حذف حسابك نحذف ملفك الشخصي ورموز الإشعارات، ونزيل بياناتك الشخصية (الاسم والبريد والموبايل والعنوان والملاحظات) من طلباتك السابقة، ونحتفظ بسجل مجهّل الهوية للطلبات (الأصناف والمبالغ والتواريخ) لأغراض محاسبية وإحصائية.

لحماية مكافآت الإحالة من تسجيل عدة حسابات جوجل على نفس هاتف أندرويد، نقرأ معرّف الجهاز المحدد للتطبيق والمستخدم ومفتاح التوقيع (ANDROID_ID)، ونرسله عبر HTTPS إلى Cloudflare الذي يحفظ بصمات HMAC مستعارة فقط لمعرّف الجهاز وحساب جوجل لمدة تصل إلى 365 يومًا. لا نخزّن رقم الجهاز الخام أو البريد أو اسم المستخدم في سجل البصمات، ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات أو للتتبع خارج وصّلي. الحسابات المختلفة تقدر تستخدم التطبيق عادي، لكن نفس الجهاز لا يستحق أكثر من مكافأة تسجيل واحدة خلال مدة الحماية. البصمات ليست بيانات مجهّلة بالكامل وتُحذف دوريًا. قد يتغير معرّف الجهاز عند ضبط المصنع أو تغيير التوقيع.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Wasally is a service for ordering products from local stores and having them delivered to your door. It is operated by wasally ("we"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Data we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Google account data when you sign in: name, email address, and an internal account ID. We never receive your Google password.
  • Delivery details you enter: name, mobile number, address, and notes.
  • Order data: store, items, quantities, prices, total, order status and time, and payment method (cash on delivery).
  • The Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token for this app installation, stored with your account to send order notifications and alerts. We remove its account association on sign-out where possible.
  • Technical data collected automatically by Google and Firebase to run and protect the service, such as IP address and device type, and an app integrity token (Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity) used to confirm that requests come from the genuine app.
  • Crash and diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics in production builds, such as app crash reports, error traces, and device-state information needed to identify and fix failures. Crashlytics collection is disabled in development builds.

Location: if you tap “Use my location”, we use your device location once to set your delivery address, and store the address and its coordinates in your account. We do not track your location in the background. Optional order attachments are deleted automatically after delivery is confirmed: a voice note (if you choose “By voice” in Quick Orders, the microphone is used only while you press the record button, up to one minute; we never record in the background) and photos of your shopping list taken with the camera or picked from your gallery (up to 3), plus receipt photos uploaded by the courier. They are hosted on Cloudinary and visible only to the assigned courier and our operations team. We only access the photo you choose yourself. On your first order we record that the “free delivery on first order” offer was used (your account ID and the order number) so it cannot be reused. Store and product images uploaded by store owners are hosted on Cloudinary.

Delivery code: a 4-digit code shown only to you in the app while your order is on its way; you give it to the courier after receiving the order to confirm delivery. The code is not stored in our database and is not visible to the courier or the store; we only record failed attempts (to prevent guessing) and the time delivery was confirmed.

Delivery issue reports: if delivery fails, the courier records a reason (for example no answer or a wrong address); the reason is shown to you, the store and our team.

Ratings: the ratings you give to stores and couriers after delivery, and an internal rating of the interaction given by the courier or store after the order is completed. Repeated negative ratings, after review by our team, may lead to a temporary account restriction.

Support and complaints: you contact us through WhatsApp on the support number shown in the app; messages there are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. Support messages and complaints sent from inside the app before that feature was retired are kept and are deleted when you delete your account.

In production, Firebase Performance Monitoring also collects app startup and operation timing, plus aggregated network request patterns. Custom traces do not attach customer IDs, order IDs, addresses, or request bodies; network patterns are aggregated and URL query parameters are excluded.

We do not collect payment card details, contacts, or your personal photos or files.

Store owners, couriers, supervisors and administrators

If you register as a store owner or courier, or are assigned a supervisor or administrator role, we may also process role-related information needed to operate the service: business and store details, addresses, images, products and prices for store owners; name, phone number, vehicle details, delivery assignments, delivery records, settlements and ratings for couriers; courier-supervision relationships for supervisors; and operational access and action records for administrators. We use this information to operate orders, deliveries, settlements, support and abuse prevention.

Store owners cannot access a customer's delivery address, phone number or email through the public order record. A courier receives contact details only for an assigned order and only during the permitted delivery stage. Supervisors can access information about couriers assigned to them as needed for supervision. Authorized administrators may access operational information needed to manage the service and resolve issues, with access restricted by role.

When a store owner, courier, supervisor or administrator deletes an account, we remove the account's role permissions, role profile and device tokens. Related records are deleted or de-identified as appropriate. If the account was the last active owner of a store, the listing is deactivated and store/catalog data may be retained temporarily for reassignment or administrative removal. Financial or operational records may be retained in de-identified form or where legally required for the applicable retention period.

How we use your data

  • To fulfil and deliver your orders and contact you about them.
  • To send notifications about your order status (you can turn them off in your device settings).
  • To save your delivery details for future orders.
  • To protect the service and prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To improve the service and produce operational statistics.

We do not sell your data and we do not show personalized ads in the app.

Who we share data with

  • The courier assigned to your order: name, mobile number, address, notes, and order contents, as needed for delivery. They can see your number and address only after taking the order and while it is in progress; access closes after delivery. Other couriers see only the area and order items.
  • The store you order from: order contents, your first name and preparation notes, plus an internal technical account identifier associated with the order for service operation and fraud prevention. This identifier may allow orders from the same account to be linked within the system. The store does not see your mobile number, delivery address or email.
  • Our operations team: to manage orders and provide support.
  • Technical service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications, crash and performance reporting), Cloudflare Workers (a server that verifies orders and prices and runs account deletion, the delivery code and notifications), and Cloudinary (image hosting), which process data on our behalf under their own policies.
  • Authorities, when required by law or a court order.

We do not share your data with anyone else for marketing purposes.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on Google Cloud and Firebase servers. Our accounts and orders database is located in Europe (Frankfurt, Germany), so your data may be transferred outside your country. Data between the app and our servers is sent over an encrypted connection.

Data retention

To limit repeat new-customer referral rewards after deletion and registration again with the same Google account, a limited fraud-prevention record is kept for 365 days after a deletion request: a secret-key HMAC-derived pseudonymous marker of the stable Google account identifier. The record contains no name, email, raw Google account identifier, or former Firebase UID. It is still linkable pseudonymous personal data, not fully anonymous. The app and stores cannot access it; it is used only to prevent repeated referral rewards, never for marketing, and is periodically deleted after its retention period.

We keep your account and order data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your profile and notification tokens, remove your personal data (name, email, mobile number, address, notes) from your past orders, and keep an anonymous record of orders (items, amounts, dates) for accounting and statistics.

To prevent multiple Google accounts on the same Android phone from repeatedly claiming new-user referral rewards, we read the Android ID scoped to the app signing key, Android user and device and transmit it over HTTPS to Cloudflare. The service stores only keyed HMAC-derived pseudonymous digests of the device and verified Google account for up to 365 days. Raw Android IDs, names and emails are not included in these records, and they are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Other accounts may use Wasally normally, but one device is eligible for only one signup referral during retention. These identifiers are not fully anonymous and are periodically deleted. Factory resets or signing changes may alter the scoped ID.
